ABOUT OUR SYSTEM

Making Library Resources Simple & Accessible

Library Seat & Resource Reservation is a centralized platform designed to make it easier for students and faculty to find, reserve and manage library resources.

What is this system?

Our Library Seat & Resource Reservation System provides a simple digital way to manage library seats and other resources.

Instead of manually checking whether a seat, study room, computer or other resource is available, users can browse resources and make reservations directly through the platform.

The system also provides a priority queue for resources that are currently reserved, allowing users to join the queue and get access when the resource becomes available.
